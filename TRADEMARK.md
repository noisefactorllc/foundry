# Foundry Trademark Policy

## 1. Purpose

This Trademark Policy ("Policy") governs the use of certain names and branding in connection with derivative works and products based on the Foundry software project. This Policy is supplemental to the MIT License under which the source code is distributed.

## 2. Scope and Limitations

**We do not claim trademark rights to the word "Foundry."** The term is generic and widely used. This Policy applies narrowly to prevent consumer confusion in contexts directly connected to this software project.

**"Noise Factor"** is a distinctive mark associated with this project and its related tooling. We ask that this term not be used in the names of derivative products.

## 3. Permitted Uses

You **may** use "Foundry" and "Noise Factor" without prior permission in the following circumstances:

- **Attribution:** Referring to the Foundry project by name in documentation, articles, tutorials, or other descriptive contexts (e.g., "This project uses Foundry" or "Built with Foundry").
- **Unmodified Distribution:** Distributing unmodified copies of the software, provided you comply with the MIT License.
- **Academic and Research Use:** Referencing the project in academic papers, presentations, or research materials.
- **Community Discussion:** Discussing the project in forums, social media, or community channels.
- **Unrelated Products:** Using "foundry" in product names for software unrelated to shader editing or effect composition.

## 4. Restricted Uses

When creating derivative works or products **based on or incorporating this software**, you **may not**:

- **Use "Noise Factor"** as the name, or part of the name, of your product, service, or derivative work.
- **Imply Official Status:** Use "Foundry" in a manner that suggests your derivative is the official Foundry project, or is endorsed by or affiliated with the Foundry maintainers.
- **Create Confusion:** Use names, logos, or branding designed to create confusion between your derivative and this project.

## 5. Derivative Works

If you create a derivative work based on this software:

- You may not use "Noise Factor" in your product name.
- If you use Foundry branding to describe or promote your derivative, include a notice that it is not the official Foundry software and is not endorsed by the project maintainers.

To request permission for uses not covered by this Policy, or to ask questions, contact [team@noisefactor.io](mailto:team@noisefactor.io).
